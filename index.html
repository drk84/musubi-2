About MissuV

MissuV is a proudly local brand offering homemade musubi—a flavorful fusion of Japanese tradition and Filipino soul, lovingly crafted to satisfy every craving. Our musubi is made using a special homemade recipe that combines soft Japanese rice, savory toppings, and a Filipino twist—like our signature Chicken Adobo and other comforting favorites. Each piece is wrapped, pressed, and prepared with care—because at MissuV, everything is made with love. But MissuV is more than just food—it’s a feeling. It’s about not missing out on the joy of flavor, the warmth of home, and the thrill of discovering your new favorite snack. Missing your go-to musubi fix? Don’t worry—you’ll be craving for Miss V to bring you more. From our kitchen to yours, thank you for letting MissuV be part of your daily cravings and delicious moments.
